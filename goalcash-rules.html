GOALCASH — Full Rules

Prototype rules. Promoter details to be completed before any live operation.

1. What GOALCASH is

GOALCASH is a prize draw. Each entry receives (a) an instant cash prize determined at random from a fixed, published prize table, and (b) one professional footballer allocated entirely at random from that day's eligible fixtures. If the allocated player scores in their fixture that day, the entry wins a fixed bonus per goal, determined by the player's position and stated on the entry at the time it is issued. Entrants exercise no choice, skill or judgement at any stage; player allocation and instant prizes are random.

2. Two ways to enter — equally effective

Online: £1 per entry at this website.

Free by post: send your full name, address, email and date of birth on a postcard or sealed letter to:

GOALCASH Free Entry, [PROMOTER ADDRESS — IGX LTD REGISTERED OFFICE]

One entry per postcard/letter; unlimited postal entries (each must be posted separately). Postal entries received by the daily cut-off are entered into the same draw for that day, receive the same random instant-prize chance and the same random player allocation from the same pool, and are eligible for identical prizes as paid entries. Postal entries are processed on the day of receipt and results notified by email. No payment to the promoter is required for postal entry.

3. Eligibility

  • Open to residents of Great Britain aged 18 or over.
  • One account/identity per person for online entry.
  • Employees of the promoter are excluded.

4. Prizes & settlement

  • Instant prizes: fixed table published on the entry page; odds independent of the number of entries sold.
  • Per-goal bonus: fixed at issue — Goalkeeper £250, Defender £25, Midfielder £5, Forward £2 per goal scored by the allocated player in the stated fixture (90 minutes plus stoppage; extra time excluded).
  • Goals are settled from the official match event timeline of our data provider. Own goals do not count. If a fixture is postponed or abandoned before completion, the per-goal bonus is settled on goals scored up to abandonment; the instant prize stands.
  • If an allocated player does not appear in the fixture, the entry's per-goal bonus simply yields £0; the instant prize element is unaffected. Players are allocated from confirmed starting line-ups where available, otherwise from full declared squads.
  • Prizes are cash, paid to the entrant's nominated account within 7 days of settlement.

5. Fair play & responsible play

  • Random allocation is performed server-side by a cryptographic RNG; no element of the outcome can be influenced by the promoter after entry.
  • This is a prize draw with a free entry route; it is not licensed gambling. If play stops being fun, stop. Support: BeGambleAware.org.
  • Self-exclusion: email the promoter and all future online entries from your account will be refused.
