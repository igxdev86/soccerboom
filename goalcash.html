Every £1 Match Pack opens 10 entries across randomly allocated fixtures from the next 72 hours of trackable football. Your player is allocated at random from the confirmed starting XIs the moment the official team sheet drops (≈1h before kickoff) — a £1 Match Pack puts 10 entries of 10p on ONE randomly allocated match — the minimum online purchase is one pack. Every entry wins cash (1p floor — every pack pays at least 10p), and each entry's footballer multiplies that entry's win: goal ×10, yellow ×3, red ×20, cumulative. Jackpot instants of £2.50, £5 and £10 are pure cash, banked on the spot. Every pack also carries a 5p Match Bonus stake multiplied by match-level drama — a hat-trick in your match ×20, hat-tricks from both teams ×1000, two reds ×15, four reds ×300, 7+ goals ×15, cumulative. Settled automatically from the official match timeline.
