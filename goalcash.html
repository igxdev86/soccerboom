How it works: every £1 ticket pays an instant cash prize (or not — most don't, that's the game) and enters you into a randomly allocated fixture from the next 72 hours of trackable football. Your player is allocated at random from the confirmed starting XIs the moment the official team sheet drops (\u22481h before kickoff) \u2014 position decides your per-goal rate. Every goal your player scores tonight pays the per-goal bonus automatically. Keepers pay £250 a goal for a reason. Tickets settle from the official match timeline after full time.

Play for free by post — every postal entry gets the identical draw, same prizes, no purchase needed.
