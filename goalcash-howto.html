How to play GOALCASH

1. Get an entry. £1 online, or completely free by post (same draw, same prizes — details).
2. Instant reveal. Every entry is checked against the fixed prize table the moment it's issued. Win cash on the spot, or not — most entries don't, and the odds are the same however many people enter.
3. Your player. Every entry also gets one footballer, picked at random from today's fixtures — confirmed starting XIs once team sheets are announced, full squads before that. You don't choose. That's the point.
4. Goals pay. Every goal your player scores in their fixture today pays the bonus printed on your entry:
PositionPer goal
Goalkeeper£250
Defender£25
Midfielder£5
Forward£2
A keeper almost never scores — that's why he's worth £250 when he does. If your player doesn't play or doesn't score, the bonus is £0 and your instant prize stands.
5. Settlement. Goals settle automatically from the official match timeline after full time (90 minutes + stoppage; extra time and own goals don't count). Winnings show on your tickets page.

18+, Great Britain residents. A prize draw, not betting — there's nothing to pick and nothing to get right. If it stops being fun, stop: BeGambleAware.org.
