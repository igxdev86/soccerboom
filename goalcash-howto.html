How to play GOALRUSH

1. Open a Match Pack. £1 buys 10 entries (the minimum online purchase), or enter completely free by post — same draw, same prizes (details).
2. Instant reveal — every ticket wins. Every entry pays a cash prize the moment it's issued, from 1p up to £10, from a fixed prize table — so every pack pays at least 10p. Jackpot instants (£2.50, £5, £10) are pure cash, banked immediately — no multiplier. Smaller prizes carry on into the multiplier game. The odds are the same however many people enter.
3. Your squad. Each of your 10 entries gets one footballer, picked at random from its fixture’s confirmed starting XI once the team sheet is announced. You don't choose. That's the point.
4. Missions multiply. Every entry is printed with a random mission and multiplier at purchase:

If your footballer hits the mission, that entry's instant cash multiplies. Example: 8p instant, SCORE + BOOKED ×100 — he scores and sees yellow, that entry pays £8. Quiet game, your instant stands. Every pack also stakes a 5p Match Bonus: hat-tricks from both teams ×1000, 4 reds ×300, 7+ goals ×15.
5. Settlement. Goals settle automatically from the official match timeline after full time (90 minutes + stoppage; extra time and own goals don't count). Winnings show on your tickets page.

Quick answers

Do I pick my match or players? No — nothing is picked by you, ever. The match and all 10 players are allocated at random. That's what makes it a prize draw, not betting.
When do my players appear? When the official team sheet drops, usually about an hour before kickoff. Until then your entries show the match and wait.
What if my match is postponed? Instant prizes always stand. Bonuses settle on whatever officially happened; a postponed match's entries are settled on the rearranged fixture where our data covers it, otherwise the instant stands alone.
When does money land? Instants at purchase, player bonuses and Match Spice shortly after full time, automatically.
