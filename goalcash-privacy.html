GOALCASH — Privacy Policy

Data controller: IGX Ltd [registered office, company number, contact email — to be completed]. This policy covers the GOALCASH prize draw.

What we collect

  • Online entries: a random device identifier (stored in your browser), entry history, prizes won. The prototype collects no name, email or payment data. The live product will additionally collect: name, email, date of birth, and payment/payout details.
  • Postal entries: name, address, email and date of birth as written on your entry.
  • Technical: standard server logs (IP address, timestamps) kept by our hosting provider for security.

Why (lawful bases)

  • Running the draw you entered and paying prizes — performance of a contract.
  • Age verification (18+) and fraud prevention — legal obligation / legitimate interests.
  • Self-exclusion list — legitimate interests / your request.

What we don't do

  • No sale of personal data. No marketing without separate opt-in consent.
  • No profiling or automated decisions beyond the random draw itself.

Sharing

  • Hosting and database providers (Vercel, Supabase) process data on our behalf.
  • Match data comes from our sports data provider; no personal data is sent to them.
  • Payment providers will process payout details when live.

Retention

  • Entry and prize records: 6 years (tax and dispute purposes).
  • Postal entry documents: destroyed after the draw is settled and any prize paid.
  • Self-exclusion records: kept for as long as the exclusion stands.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under UK GDPR — email [contact]. Complaints: Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk.
